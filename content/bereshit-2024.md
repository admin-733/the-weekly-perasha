The Torah doesn't begin with laws. It doesn't begin with history. It begins with creation.

Why?

Because before you can follow rules or learn from the past, you need to understand something fundamental: **you have the power to create.**

## The Morning Reset

Every morning, Hashem renews creation. The Sages teach that the world isn't a machine set in motion long ago — it's actively being sustained, moment by moment. If Hashem renews the world every day, then every day is genuinely new. Yesterday's failures don't define today.

Think about what that means practically. You had a terrible day at work? Tomorrow is literally a new creation. You lost your temper with your kids? Tonight you can start fresh.

This isn't just positive thinking — it's how the world actually works according to the Torah.

## The Trap of "It's Too Late"

One of the most destructive thoughts a person can have is *"It's too late for me."* Too late to change careers. Too late to fix a relationship. Too late to start learning.

Bereshit rejects that entirely. The world was "formless and void" — complete chaos — and from that, Hashem created everything. If order can come from chaos on a cosmic level, it can certainly happen in your life.

## Your Practical Takeaway

This week, pick one area of your life where you've been telling yourself "it's too late" or "I can't change this." Just one. And take the smallest possible step toward creating something new in that area.

You don't need to overhaul your life. Bereshit teaches us that creation happens in stages — day by day, step by step. Start with Day One.
